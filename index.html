Chapter 04 · The Impact

The Changemakers

Founded in Kandahar in 2015 as Change in Youths Social Assembly (+CYSA) and formally registered in 2018, the organization brought young people together for education, rights awareness and community service. After August 2021 it adapted and continued as The Changemakers.

  • Human rights
  • Youth leadership
  • Women’s empowerment
