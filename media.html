150 words

Muhammad Salim Daqiq is an entrepreneur, organizational founder and development professional working at the intersection of international business, institutional leadership and social transformation. In 2015 he founded Change in Youths Social Assembly (+CYSA) in Kandahar, a youth-led civil society organization that was formally registered in 2018 and works for human rights, youth leadership, women’s empowerment and girls’ education; after August 2021 it continued as The Changemakers. He is Founder and Executive Leader of DAQIQ INTERNATIONAL, an international trading initiative in import and export, global sourcing, agricultural and food products and industrial supplies. His professional experience includes humanitarian logistics with the World Food Programme (2021–2024), executive coordination at the Kandahar Directorate of Public Health (2024–2025) and psychosocial support work. He has founded more than five English-language and computer-training academies. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Agricultural Science from the University of Kandahar.
