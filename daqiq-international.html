Overview

Trade built on trust.

DAQIQ International is the entrepreneurial and international trading initiative founded and led by Muhammad Salim Daqiq. It brings together sourcing, supply and distribution for businesses that need reliable partners across borders.

How we work

Standards before volume.

01

Specification first

Every engagement starts with the buyer’s exact requirements: product, quality, quantity, timing and budget.

02

Verified suppliers

Suppliers are compared and checked before any commitment, so partners know who they are dealing with.

03

Compliance

Counterparties are screened against international sanctions lists, and every transaction follows applicable trade rules.

04

Transparent relationships

Clear terms, clear documentation and open communication from enquiry to delivery.
