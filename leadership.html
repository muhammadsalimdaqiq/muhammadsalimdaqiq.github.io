Leadership is institution building.

Founder journey

A founder’s path through youth mobilization, education, humanitarian work and enterprise, and the principles that hold it together.

2015

The first assembly

Young volunteers in Kandahar come together as Change in Youths Social Assembly (+CYSA), focused on education, rights awareness and community service.

2015 — 2018

From initiative to institution

Volunteer mobilization, English and computer classes, human-rights awareness and relationships with women-led groups lead to organizational systems and formal registration in 2018.

2018 — 2021

Community practice

Programmes in hygiene and sanitation advocacy, a free community learning centre, health promotion with nomadic communities and a volunteer network.

2021

Adaptation

After August 2021, direct community activities were interrupted. The organization reviewed its identity and approach and continued as The Changemakers.

Parallel path

Academies and enterprise

More than five English-language and computer-training academies; and today DAQIQ International, applying the same founder discipline to international trade.

Now

Renewal

Strengthening governance and partnerships, and developing a women’s economic-empowerment initiative.

Leadership principles

Six principles behind every organization he builds.

Shared ownership

Organizations belong to their volunteers, members and communities, not to one person.

Integrity & accountability

Accurate records and honest reporting, internally and to every partner.

Inclusion

Women and young people are designed in from the start, not added later.

Human dignity

Every programme and transaction respects the people it touches.

Continuous learning

Measure outcomes, not only attendance, and adapt to what is learned.

Sustainability

Favour livelihoods and institutions that can stand on their own.
