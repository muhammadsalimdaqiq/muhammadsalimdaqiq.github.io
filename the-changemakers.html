The Changemakers

Pillar 03 · Formerly Change in Youths Social Assembly (+CYSA)

A community-rooted civil society organization, founded in Kandahar in 2015, working for education, human rights, women’s empowerment and youth development.

2021Became The Changemakers
FounderMuhammad Salim Daqiq
Our story

Founded by youth. Sustained by community.

The Changemakers is the continuing institutional journey of Change in Youths Social Assembly (+CYSA). Its history belongs to the volunteers, members, community groups and partners who built it alongside its founder.

Change · Youth · Social · Assembly · Action

Organizational philosophy
2015

Founded

Established in Kandahar by young people working in the development sector, as Change in Youths Social Assembly (+CYSA).

2018

Registered

Formally registered in Afghanistan, with organizational systems and administrative structures in place.

2018 — 2021

Community practice

Hygiene and sanitation advocacy; a free community education and learning centre; health promotion with nomadic communities; a volunteer and activist network; youth participation and advocacy.

Aug 2021

Transition

Direct community activities were interrupted. The organization reviewed its identity and operating approach.

Since 2021

The Changemakers

Continuing the journey under a new public identity, with a broader social and community-service orientation.

Today

Renewal

Rebuilding around women’s livelihoods, learning and skills, child protection, community well-being and stronger governance.

Vision & mission

Opportunity, knowledge and dignity for everyone.

V

Vision

A society in which every person, whatever their social or economic circumstances, has access to opportunity, knowledge, dignity and participation, and the means to create positive change.

M

Mission

To empower young people, women and underserved communities through education, capacity building, volunteerism, rights awareness, community participation and sustainable livelihoods.

✦

Values

Human dignity · Inclusion and equal opportunity · Volunteerism and participation · Integrity and accountability · Learning · Sustainability

Proposed initiative

Women’s Economic Empowerment through Fruit Drying & Processing, Kandahar.

A women-centred production unit planned for an initial group of 20 women, with training in drying and processing, hygiene and food safety, packaging, business skills and market access. The initiative is in development and seeking partners; it is not yet funded or operating.
