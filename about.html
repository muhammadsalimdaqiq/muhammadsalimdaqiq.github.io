Executive biography

Entrepreneur, organizational founder and development professional working at the intersection of international business, institutional leadership and social transformation.

Biography

Muhammad Salim Daqiq is an Afghan entrepreneur and founder whose work spans international trade, institution building and community development. He is the Founder and Executive Leader of DAQIQ INTERNATIONAL and the founder of The Changemakers, the organization he established in 2015 as Change in Youths Social Assembly (+CYSA).

Trained in agricultural science at University of Kandahar and in business administration at the Sherzai Institute, he combines an understanding of agriculture and commodities with practical management. Early on he turned that combination toward education, founding and developing more than five English-language and computer-training academies that gave young people in southern Afghanistan access to language skills and digital literacy.

Founder of a youth movement

In 2015 he brought together young volunteers in Kandahar to form +CYSA, which was formally registered in 2018. The organization worked for human rights awareness, youth leadership, women’s empowerment and girls’ education, and ran community programmes in education, hygiene and health promotion. After the changes in Afghanistan in August 2021 it adapted its identity and approach and continued as The Changemakers.

Operational discipline

His professional career has given him operational and institutional depth. From 2021 to 2024 he served as a Logistics Assistant with the World Food Programme, supporting humanitarian supply chains with careful documentation, reporting and accountability. From 2024 to 2025 he was Executive Officer and Secretary at the Kandahar Directorate of Public Health, responsible for executive coordination, official communication and administration. Since 2025 he has worked in psychosocial support, with a focus on dignity and beneficiary-centred communication.

The enterprise

Through DAQIQ INTERNATIONAL he is building an international trading initiative in import and export, global sourcing, agricultural and food products, industrial supplies and wholesale distribution, grounded in trust, compliance and long-term partnerships.
